# Safety

Year of the Horse inherits The Hero's Journey's checks, unchanged: the WCAG 2.3.1 flash and red-flash analyser, the regular-pattern analyser after the ITC (Ofcom) guidance, whole-field motion caps from optical flow, red-capped colour, the start gate and held frames. Their rules, sources and limits are written up in [The Hero's Journey's docs/safety.md](../../herosjourney/docs/safety.md). Every effect and preset here runs through them in `npm test`, at the default and at the strong dose (0.7).

The hazards this family expects are in [plan.md](../plan.md): bursts and salutes as flashes, strobe and glitter stars and firecracker strings as rapid flashing, Lunar New Year's saturated red, chasing bulbs and scrolling LED text as moving patterns, neon flicker as it strikes and fails, and bursts expanding as a zoom.

What this family has found so far:

- **A burst's expansion reads as a zoom.** On a dark sky the motion check can only track the stars, so one expanding burst is the whole field it fits. With stars slowed gently (drag 1.5 a second), the median zoom over a run was over the cap. Stars that stop within about a second (drag 3 to 3.6 a second), quicker than real ones, pass with three shells in the air; the strong dose is held to four. Whether a burst in part of the view is whole-field motion at all is plan story 2.8.
- **Falling stars read as drift.** Stars falling at a third to half of the burst's radius a second read 0.06 to 0.1 screen widths a second, over the cap of 0.05. Real stars fall at about a thirteenth to a sixth of the radius a second (2.1's model), and that passes.
- **A burst of thick streaks is a grating.** Streaks a fixed 1.6 px wide in the 480 × 270 test frame (taken as a 1920-pixel view) read pattern `high`. Drawn in proportion to the burst, a pixel wide with their light kept where they would be thinner, they read `none`.
- **A red peony is a red flash.** One analyser cell flashed red once in four seconds as a strontium-red burst lit and burnt out in it: flash `low`, declared, far under three a second.
- **A burst's growth is read exactly, when it is read at all** (story 2.8, [studies/burst-safety.md](studies/burst-safety.md)). Whenever the estimator can track a burst, its zoom reading is the burst's own growth, 0.4 to 1.1 doublings a second for its first 1.5 s; off-centre it is also a drift. Bursts under a twentieth of the view's width are never seen. Displays pass by being invisible most of the time; `willow`, whose dense tails are trackable, cannot, and is declared `motionDefaults`: every setting starts behind the gate, the gallery holds it still, and the notice offers no calm version. A reading for local looming is proposed in the study, for the shared rules to decide.
- **Salutes: three a second.** A white break of any size passes the flash rule at three a second and fails at four. `display`'s finale breaks every 0.4 s, and its whole loop reads `low`.
- **A long exposure of willows is a grating.** With the camera's trails, dozens of near-parallel drooping arcs read pattern `high` (0.345 of the view), held frames too. `willow` offers only the eye's trails.
- **The flash analyser cannot see sparkle.** Glitter flashes, crackle pops and strobe stars are a pixel or two each, under cells 80 CSS px across, and every preset of `glitter-finale` reads `low` or `none`, a 12 Hz strobe at the strong dose included. That is a blind spot, not a measurement of safety: the shimmer test is still to come, and `full-strobe` is declared and gated anyway.
