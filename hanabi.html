EXPERIMENT 01 / HANABI OVER THE RIVER

Everykindof shell.

A summer night's display over a river, after the hanabi taikai on the Sumida. Each kind of shell gets an act of its own: peonies, chrysanthemums, the hand-packed warimono, rings, palms, willows, crossettes that split, glitter, crackle and strobes, shells that draw shapes, then all of them together and a finale. The smoke drifts on the wind, lit by the bursts that follow, and the river mirrors it all.

HOW THE SHOW WORKS

Every star is
a moment in time.

01 / FLIGHT

No simulation.

Each star's path is worked out ahead of time from how a real star flies: fast off the break, slowed by the air, pulled down by gravity. Any frame can be drawn directly from the time, so the show can pause, hold or resize without losing its place.

02 / TRAILS

As the eye sees it.

Each streak is drawn back along the star's own path, as long as the eye holds a bright light. Switch the trails to a camera's and they stretch to a long exposure, as in a photograph of fireworks.

03 / SMOKE AND WATER

Lit from inside.

Every break leaves a puff of smoke that drifts and thins on the wind and glows in the colour of the bursts behind it. The river below mirrors the sky, rippled and stretched into streaks.
